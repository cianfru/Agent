How it works

Logged before the outcome

A call is written down with the time and the price when the desk makes it, before anyone knows how it goes. It shows here after a delay; holders of the token see calls as they happen.

Scored by the clock

Each call is scored at exactly 1, 3 and 7 days from when it was logged, from Hyperliquid's price at that moment, not when someone looked. Tracked traders' positions also at 14 and 30.

Nothing taken out

A call is never edited or deleted. One that should not count is voided, with its reason, and stays on this page. Calls logged before the chain began are marked backfilled.

Context, not advice. Past calls say nothing certain about the next one. Data · Chain
